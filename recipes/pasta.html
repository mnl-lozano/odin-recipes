Pasta with cherry tomatoes

Bonjour! Today I'm going to show you my unbelievable cherry tomato pasta recipe. Hope you enjoy it!

Ingredients

  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Pasta (preferably fusilli)
  • Extra Virgin Olive Oil
  • Garlic
  • Black Pepper
  • Salt

Steps

  1. First we need to fry the garlic lightly on olive oil. We only need around 1 minute; the key is that we heat it up enough for the garlic to release its aroma without burning, which would develop a bitter taste
  2. Now we can remove the garlic from the pan and fry the tomatoes face down. Once they become soft we can start smashing them with our spatula to create the sauce. In the meantime, we can start cooking our pasta
  3. Once the pasta is nearly cooked we can mix it with the sauce making sure we also include some pasta water and mix vigorously. This step is called "mantecare la pasta" and it's absolutely key
